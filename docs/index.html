How it works

Compare, don't just count

Most AI ROI figures multiply self-reported minutes by a salary. This tool compares people who got the AI tool with a similar group who did not, before and after rollout.

Remove what would have happened anyway

A quieter month or a new process makes everyone faster. Subtracting the comparison group's change (difference-in-differences) keeps that out of the AI's credit.

Show the uncertainty

Every estimate comes with a 95% range. A saving is only called proven when the whole range is above zero.

Guard quality

Speed that creates rework is not a win. If rework rises past your guardrail, the verdict is Fix, however fast the tool is.

How verdicts are decided

VerdictWhen
KeepThe time saving is proven, the tool pays for itself, at least 30% of eligible people use it, and quality held.
FixSomething specific is wrong: too few people use it, the saving is not proven yet, its value does not cover its cost, or quality slipped.
RetireNo evidence it saves time, or it costs more than it returns even in the best case.
